BONE ROAD

EMBERBROOK · WHISPERING WOOD · MIREFEN BOG · HOLLOW GRAVES · AND HOME AGAIN
  • You are a skeleton adventurer. Walk the road out of Emberbrook, through the forest, the swamp and the graveyard, and back into town.
  • Press ROLL (or Space) to throw two dice and move as many squares as they add up to.
  • A beam of light marks the square your roll takes you to. Only the square you stop on counts. Red squares hide a foe: land on one and you cut it down for 10 gold.
  • Gold squares hold a chest. Grey iron plates are spike traps and cost a heart.
  • After every turn, one new monster steps out of the forest, the swamp and the graveyard onto an empty square ahead of you.
  • Teal squares are shrines. Each one restores a heart, once.
  • Run out of hearts and the road claims you. Reach the gold flag in Emberbrook to finish; you don't need an exact roll.
  • Drag to look around, wheel or pinch to zoom, and Q / E or the arrow buttons to turn the camera.
