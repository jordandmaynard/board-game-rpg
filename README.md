# Bone Road

A fantasy RPG board game with the same low-res, isometric pixel look as [Dungeon Golf](https://github.com/jordandmaynard/dungeon-golf).

A skeleton adventurer walks a 91-square road out of the town of Emberbrook, through the Whispering Wood, Mirefen Bog and the Hollow Graves, and home again.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. It is a single file; Three.js r128 loads from cdnjs.

## Rules

- **Move:** press ROLL (or Space) to throw two dice and walk that many squares. A beam of light marks where you will stop. Only that square counts.
- **Foes** (red squares): landing on one cuts it down at once for 10 gold. Goblins and wolves in the wood, slimes in the bog, ghosts, zombies and skeletons in the graves.
- **Chests** (gold squares): gold inside, once.
- **Spike traps** (iron plates): cost a heart.
- **Shrines** (teal squares): restore a heart, once.
- **Wandering monsters:** after every turn the wood, the bog and the graves each send one monster out of the scenery onto an empty square ahead of you.
- You start with 5 hearts. Reach the gold flag in Emberbrook to win; an exact roll isn't needed.

## Controls

- **Roll:** ROLL button, Space or Enter.
- **Camera:** drag to look around, wheel or pinch to zoom, Q / E or the arrow buttons to turn.
